FAQ

Questions before you book

Is the price per person or per group?

Per group. The price covers the whole private vehicle for up to six guests, so the more you are, the less each person pays. The 4-day Swiss Dream Tour is priced per person.

What is included?

Private hotel pickup and drop-off, the Mercedes V-Class with a professional driver and tour assistance, fuel, parking and road costs, and on most tours a private picnic.

What is not included?

Cable cars, mountain trains, boats and entrance tickets (listed on each tour), plus tips and personal expenses.

Where do you pick us up?

At your hotel or accommodation in Lucerne, Zurich, Interlaken or nearby. The Lake Geneva tour starts from Geneva or Lausanne.

Can we change the stops?

Yes. Add or remove stops and change the schedule. The price may change with bigger changes.

What happens if the weather is bad?

Mountain weather changes fast. We can suggest a nearby alternative, adjust the times or move the tour to another date. If severe weather or an official closure makes the tour unsafe, we look for an alternative or a refund under our cancellation policy.

How do we book and pay?

Send a request on WhatsApp or by e-mail. We confirm in writing, and a 20% deposit secures the booking. Prices are in Swiss francs (CHF).

Can we cancel?

Yes, free of charge up to 15 days before the tour. After that the fee is only a share of the 20% deposit.

We are more than six people.

Write to us. For larger groups we arrange a suitable vehicle on request.
