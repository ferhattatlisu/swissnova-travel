FAQ

Transfers

Which airports do you serve?

Zurich, Geneva and Basel, and other selected airports on request.

Can I book a driver for a full day?

Yes. Full-day and multi-day arrangements are possible for sightseeing, business and private travel.

Do you drive to Zermatt?

Zermatt is car-free, so road transfers end in Täsch. Onward travel to Zermatt is arranged separately.

Do you drive abroad?

Selected trips to Italy, France, Germany and Austria can be requested, subject to confirmation.

Do you have child seats?

Tell us the ages of the children when you ask for a quote and we plan the seats.

How is the price calculated?

By route, vehicle, date, number of guests, duration and extra requests. You receive an individual quote before you book.

Early morning or late at night?

Yes, on request. Availability and any extra charge are confirmed with the quote.
